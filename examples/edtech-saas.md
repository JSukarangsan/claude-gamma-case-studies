# EdTech SaaS Platform: 340% Increase in Sales Qualified Leads with AI-Powered Prospect Intelligence

How a mid-market education technology company transformed their sales process with automated research and intelligent lead scoring

---

## THE CHALLENGE

A B2B EdTech SaaS company selling learning management software to K-12 school districts was struggling with inefficient prospecting. Their sales team of 12 reps spent 8-10 hours per week manually researching school districts—digging through state databases, budget reports, board meeting minutes, and news articles to identify districts with budget availability and technology initiatives. Lead scoring was entirely manual and inconsistent across reps. With a 6-month sales cycle and $50K-$200K deal sizes, wasting time on unqualified leads meant missed quota and frustrated sales teams.

---

## THE SOLUTION

We built an AI-powered prospect research and lead scoring system that automated the entire qualification process. The system monitored state education databases, local news sources, school board meeting minutes, and district technology plans. Claude AI analyzed this unstructured data to identify buying signals: recent bond approvals, technology director hires, LMS contract expirations, and curriculum modernization initiatives. An N8N workflow orchestrated data collection from multiple sources, scored leads based on 23 qualified criteria, and enriched CRM records with strategic talking points. Each morning, sales reps received a prioritized list of warm prospects with AI-generated research briefs.

---

## THE RESULTS

• Sales Qualified Leads increased by 340% (from 15 to 66 per month)
• Research time reduced from 8 hours to 45 minutes per week per rep
• Win rate improved from 12% to 31% on qualified opportunities
• Average sales cycle shortened from 6.2 months to 3.8 months (39% faster)
• Revenue per rep increased by $180,000 annually
• Sales team capacity expanded from 85 to 280 active prospects
• Implementation completed in 9 weeks
